What counts

Every bound from the known-machines registry is credited to its finder. The counts and steps of a square drawn in a status colour come from our own run of that cell.

A halter is run to its halt, a non-halter carries a cycler or translated-cycler certificate (Coq-BB5's Loop decider), an n-gram CPS certificate, a repeated-word-list certificate, a finite-automata-reduction certificate or a history n-gram CPS certificate, and any other machine stays a holdout. A cell shows as open or resolved only once a second program, written from CHECKING.md alone, agrees on its count, every halt and every certificate. The results are informal, in the community's sense. Outside the squares, a † marks a number credited to others. It is shown as their claim and not checked here. A violet, blue or orange square rests on other people's proven values or holdout lists, which an import checks against our run (CHECKING.md section 13).
