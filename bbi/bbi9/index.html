Daniel Yuan's BB(2,5) champion holds the BBi(9) record, the longest known run of any Turing machine with nine instructions, at more than 10↑↑4 steps. Nick Drozd proposed working through every nine-instruction machine. This page sorts them by shape, the number of states and symbols they use, so that each cell can be settled on its own.

The census

The census counts 926,173,457,576 nine-instruction machines whose eight-instruction parent halts within 100,000 steps. The eight-instruction machines it left open add at most 889,868,852 more. Shorter machines it left open never halt, because each ran 4,000,000 steps, past the proven value of BBi(7).

What counts

A halter is run to its halt, a non-halter carries a cycler or translated-cycler certificate (Coq-BB5's Loop decider), an n-gram CPS certificate, a repeated-word-list certificate or a finite-automata-reduction certificate, and any other machine stays a holdout. A cell shows as open or resolved only once a second program, written from CHECKING.md alone, agrees on its count, every halt and every certificate. The results are informal, in the community's sense. A † marks a number credited to others or derived from their results. It is shown as their claim and not checked here.

Reproducing a cell

Anyone can check a cell whose page posts its claims file without trusting us. The checker is one Python file written from CHECKING.md, and it reads a cell's files and the census.

python bbi9check.py <a cell's directory> --census census.tsv

Each cell's page names the version of our producer that made its claims, and the checker verifies them without it.

Method

  • Nick Drozd proposed the project.
  • Allen Brady's Tree Normal Form generates the machines.
  • Coq-BB5 proves the cycler and translated-cycler method in its Loop decider. Its NGramCPS decider is the model for n-gram CPS, and its RepWL decider, in spirit, for repeated word lists.
  • The bbchallenge deciders paper and Justin Blanchard's decider describe finite automata reduction, which Coq-BB5 verifies in Verifier_FAR.
  • The bbchallenge wiki's BBi tables give the counts the census reproduces.
